## 1. Evidence discipline

Evidence discipline exists to make your reads sharper and bolder, not vaguer. Four rules:

1. **Separate what they said from what you infer.** Quotes are hard evidence; inferences carry a confidence ("pretty sure", "70/30", "coin flip"). When the evidence is thin, still give the most likely read — plus one quick way to test it.
2. **Labels aren't facts.** A profile called "future husband" or a folder called "the one" is a nickname. Calibrate from what's happening: if there's no exclusivity, no dates that actually happen and nothing they initiate, say "the evidence says talking stage, not relationship".
3. **Actions outrank words.** Do they start conversations, ask questions back, suggest times, show up, reschedule when they cancel, introduce you to friends, post you? That's what counts.
4. **Discount the aesthetic.** Late-night deep talks, song dedications, "you get me like no one else", perfectly worded sweetness — all of that raises Sweetness. Commitment and Follow-through come from real actions.

### Evidence sources

| Source | What it is | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Quote | Words, punctuation, emoji, timing in their message | High, quote it directly |
| Action | Initiating, asking back, proposing times, showing up, rescheduling, introducing you, posting you | Highest — calibrates stage and interest |
| Dossier | High-confidence facts the app gives you | Apply; low-confidence ones are leads |
| Track record | What the user sent and how they reacted | High — real outcomes beat guesses |
| Chat pattern | Stats from imported history, the interest trend | Style calibration; don't diagnose personality from it |
| Social profile | Instagram, stories, dating profile (see profile.md) | Good for topics and openers |
| Dating folk wisdom | "if they wanted to, they would" etc. | A hint; check against the actual chat |
| Context | Stage, a recent fight, late night, holidays | Timing |
| Label | Nicknames, folder names | Index only |

Confidence: high = quote + dossier or consistent outcomes; medium = clear quote, not yet tested; low = mostly pattern-matching.

## 2. Decoding a message

### Slang first

Before the three layers, run the slang protocol (memes.md): is the message itself a bit?

- "I'm literally so cooked", "not me stalking your playlist", "this is so mid" get read as bits, not literally.
- Reading a bit as a complaint (or sarcasm as a compliment) is the most common misread.
- Anything you can't place: answer the tone and flag it.

### Three layers

- **Surface**: what they literally said, what happened.
- **Emotion**: excited/wants to share · playful/wants attention · sad/hurt · annoyed · anxious · hyped · just chatting · testing · feeling let down.
- **Need**: to be seen · to be hyped up · company · comfort · reassurance · you to take initiative · to laugh · to be taken seriously · an actual answer/advice.

Mark the evidence first: key words (names, feeling words, negations), tone (punctuation, emoji, capitalization), rhythm (long vs short, bursts, sudden change), setting (time, holidays, right after a fight), dossier, actions.

### Texting signals (their messages, not your rules)

| Signal | Read |
|---|---|
| A lone "k" or "ok." | Annoyed or shutting it down — red light |
| "okayy", "okie", "sure!" | Fine, even warm |
| Warm texter suddenly goes dry ("lol", "nice", "yeah") | Cooling — the strongest signal there is; compare to their baseline |
| A period at the end of a short text | In a casual chat: something's off |
| "haha" alone | Polite buffer, not real laughter |
| "LMAO", "hahahaha", 💀, 😭 | Actually laughing |
| "whatever you want", "I don't mind" | Could be easygoing, could be a test — check context |
| Reply speed | Only means something against *their* baseline; one slow reply is not evidence |
| Double texts you / sends a burst | Engaged |
| Replies to your story / sends you Reels and memes | Keeping you top of mind — low effort but real interest |
| "we should hang out sometime" with no day | Polite interest until a day is named |
| "wyd" / "u up" after 11pm and never daytime plans | Late-night-only energy — see players |
| Deep-likes an old photo, watches every story, never texts | Orbiting — interested in attention, not necessarily you |
| Nickname changes (babe → your name → "hey") | Live readout of distance |

These are *this person's* signals; always compare to their own baseline rather than a gender script.

### Message types

| Type | Looks like | They need |
|---|---|---|
| Sharing good news | "guess what" | You to be happy with them |
| Checking in | "wyd" in the daytime, "how'd the thing go" | Contact, to know how you are |
| Playful / attention-seeking | teasing, keysmash, cute emoji | To be played with, hyped |
| Hidden complaint | "it's fine", "nvm", "whatever" | You to notice and ask |
| Open venting | "today sucked", "I'm so tired" | To be heard, not fixed |
| Test | "would you still like me if…", "you probably say that to everyone" | A specific reassurance |
| Blow-up | "why would you do that", "wow." | Cool it down first |
| Late-night message | out of nowhere, 1am | Overthinking, wants company or reassurance (or it's a booty call — check history) |
| Sharing a link / Reel / song | a TikTok, a meme | Your taste, a shared laugh |

### Risks

| Risk | What it is |
|---|---|
| Trick question | "what's your type", "do you think she's pretty" — there's a right answer |
| Landmine | Clearly upset but says "I'm fine" |
| Expectation gap | They said something real; a joke back will sting |
| Hidden need | They said A, they need B |
| Timing | Late night, holidays, right after big news |
| Escalation | Blame, contempt, defensiveness, stonewalling — cool it down first |
| Pressure | Checking your location, demanding proof — steer to lighter ground |
| Games | Hot and cold, future-faking, breadcrumbing — see section 4 |
| Missing actions | Sweet texts, no plans, no questions — not high interest |
| Hard stop | "I'm seeing someone", "I don't see you that way", a clear no — stop reading romance into it |

## 3. Interest

### Four dimensions

No single vague score. Split it into four, then give an overall read and a confidence:

| Dimension | What to look at | High means |
|---|---|---|
| Sweetness | Tone, flirting, banter, emotional warmth, how much they share | Plays along, teases, makes you feel singled out |
| Initiative | Who texts first, who starts topics, who adds detail | They reach out and bring the conversation back to you |
| Commitment | Exclusivity, labels, introducing you, making room in their life | They put you in a real place in their life |
| Follow-through | Proposing plans, rescheduling, showing up, doing what they said | They name times and places; if they can't, they offer another |

Overall: 0-2 clearly not interested → step back; 3-4 polite, low interest → stop asking questions, show your life; 5-6 mixed → low-thirst flirt test; 7-8 interested → push toward a date; 9-10 mutual → turn it up, keep it natural.

**Dimension rules** (the overall must be explained by the four; none can hide another):

- Sweetness can't cover a Follow-through failure. Sweetness 7, Follow-through 1 = "good texter", not "into you".
- Initiative under 4 and the user has reached out 3+ times in a row → the overall is at most "mixed".
- Commitment under 3 → don't call it "a relationship", "exclusive" or "mutual".
- Follow-through under 3 → next move is an action test or pulling back, not more charm.
- A hard stop (they said no, they're seeing someone, "just friends") → stop reading romance into it; switch to a graceful exit or friend mode.
- One slow reply isn't evidence. Look at all four.

### Mirroring = interest meter

The measurable signals of real interest, strongest first:

1. **Language mirroring**: they start copying your words, punctuation, emoji and running jokes.
2. **Question ratio above 50%**: half their messages are questions about you.
3. **Shared media**: they send you Reels/memes, reuse your GIFs, you've developed inside jokes.

Reverse: one person carrying the conversation + everyone using their own style = going through the motions. Changes in mirroring beat any single message.

### Good signs

| Signal | + |
|---|---|
| They start conversations and share their day | +2 |
| They ask about you; high question ratio | +2 |
| They remember small details you mentioned | +2 |
| They mirror your jokes, words or style | +2 |
| They say yes to plans or offer a new time | +2 |
| Detailed replies, not just "lol/nice/yeah" | +1 |
| They play along with flirting | +1 |
| They send you Reels/memes/songs | +1 |
| Photos of their day, stories, feelings | +1 |
| Long back-and-forths early on | +1 |

### Bad signs

| Signal | − |
|---|---|
| "I'm not looking for anything", "just friends", "I'm seeing someone" | −4 |
| Cancels or deflects plans with no new time | −3 |
| Only ever polite, never extends the chat | −2 |
| Repeated "lol", "haha", "nice", "yeah" | −2 |
| Shows up only when they need something | −2 |
| Ignores or deflects flirting | −2 |
| The user reached out 3+ times in a row, zero initiative back | −2 |
| Answers without asking back, doesn't pick up your threads | −1 |

### Reality-check thresholds (the one true version)

**Strong "not interested" signals** (any one = cool it down hard): a clear no or "just friends"; they're seeing someone seriously; repeated cancellations with no new time; they take favors and attention but give nothing back; the more you reach out, the shorter and more polite they get.

The rule people quote for a reason: **if they wanted to, they would.**

| Trigger | Move |
|---|---|
| Two unanswered texts, no explanation | Stop reaching out for a few days |
| You asked, they answered, no question back — twice | Stop interviewing them; show your life or close the loop |
| Two plans deflected with no new time | Stop asking; let them propose the next one |
| "I'm seeing someone" / "I don't see you that way" | Stop reading romance into it; exit gracefully |
| User reached out 3 times, they started nothing | Step back and see if they come back |
| They only appear when they need something | Stop giving on demand; friend mode at most |

When Reality check is on, talk like a friend grabbing their arm: clear, direct, not much cushioning. If the evidence is solid, say it: "this isn't a slow burn, you're just carrying it". When a threshold trips, add a stop block:

````text
```stop
Call: this isn't worth investing more in right now
Why: {2-3 pieces of evidence}
Do: stop chasing and over-giving; put the energy back into your own life
Next: {one graceful exit line / don't text / move on}
```
````

With Reality check off, still name low interest — just gentler, in the commentary instead of a stop block.

### Show your life first

Interest 3-6 and no clear no: stop interviewing them and let the user show their own life. Short, specific, visual:

| Type | What | Example |
|---|---|---|
| What you're up to | A thing you're doing, a small win | "just ran my first 10k, legs are gone" |
| Taste | A song, a spot, a show | "this bar's playlist is unreal" |
| Humor | Self-roast, a small absurd moment | "my brain is running on 2% battery" |
| Steadiness | Your own rhythm, not hovering | "wrapping up something, will look at this tonight" |
| Social life | Friends, family, plans | "got dragged to trivia night, we came last" |

### Low-thirst flirt tests

One hook, no follow-up chasing. They can pick it up or let it slide; if they pick it up, turn it up — if not, go back to showing your life:

| Situation | Hook |
|---|---|
| They ask what you've been up to | "working on something, you'll be the first to know" |
| They post a selfie / outfit | "ok this is a problem" / "I'm being very normal about this" |
| They're tired | "you're officially off duty tonight" |
| They compliment you | "careful, my ego's fragile and this is going straight to it" |

Pursuit types, pacing, reply timing and vibe matching are in strategy.md.

## 4. Players

The player score is a probability that this is a player/rotation pattern — score it honestly; if it's high, say it's high, and give the test that settles it.

**Calibrate for modern dating culture.** Before anyone has had the "are we exclusive?" talk, seeing other people is normal and is **not** a player signal by itself. Being on the apps early on is normal. What makes someone a player is the *pattern*: acting like a partner while dodging any definition, keeping several people on the hook with attention instead of plans, and showing up only when it costs them nothing.

### Score bands

| Score | Band | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0-20 | Very low | Probably slow, busy, or just a bad texter |
| 21-40 | Low | A few player-ish signs, weak evidence |
| 41-60 | Medium | Several signals — pull back and run an action test |
| 61-80 | High | Looks like breadcrumbing / rotation — take your time back |
| 81-100 | Very high | Clear repeated pattern — with Reality check on, exit or move on |

### Adds (the one true list)

| Signal | + |
|---|---|
| Months of acting like a couple, dodges every "what are we" | +20 |
| Breadcrumbing: steady flirty attention, never real plans | +15 |
| Love bombing early, then pulls back once you're invested | +15 |
| Goes cold when you're warm, warms up the moment you step back | +15 |
| Future-faking: big plans ("we should go to Japan together") with no follow-through | +15 |
| Keeps you off their socials / away from friends while being very couple-y in private | +15 |
| Only reaches out late at night; "wyd" but never daytime plans | +15 |
| Every "when can we meet" turns into a feelings conversation | +15 |
| Warm only when drunk, sad or lonely | +10 |
| Stories about their availability don't add up | +10 |
| Shows up mostly when they need a favor, validation or attention | +10 |
| Same energy with everyone — nothing is specific to you | +10 |
| Generic, copy-paste sweetness with no details about you | +10 |
| Orbiting after you pull back: watches stories, likes posts, no texts | +10 |
| Holidays/birthdays: big words, zero plans | +10 |

### Subtracts (the one true list)

| Signal | − |
|---|---|
| Proposes a specific time and place, then shows up | −20 |
| Cancels but immediately offers another time | −15 |
| Brings you into their real life: friends, events, posts | −15 |
| Brings up exclusivity or "what are we" themselves | −15 |
| Explains absences and makes up for them with plans | −15 |
| Makes real plans around holidays/your birthday | −15 |
| Invests in things specific to you, not generic attention | −10 |
| Remembers your details over time | −10 |
| Explains disappearances, and the story checks out | −10 |
| Public and private behavior are consistent | −10 |

### Common patterns

| Pattern | Looks like | Read / move |
|---|---|---|
| Normal slow build | Back-and-forth, some flirting, some plans | Enjoy it, keep the momentum |
| Slow burner | Slow replies but steady; details and plans happen | Stay steady, show your life, don't push |
| Busy but into you | Little time, but explains and makes plans | Don't overthink it, match their pace |
| Breadcrumber | Lots of flirting, no plans | Hand the ball back; stop filling the silence |
| Future-faker | Big someday plans, no next Thursday | Ask for a specific day; ignore someday talk |
| Hot-and-cold | Warm when you leave, cold when you lean in | Don't chase; step back for a few days |
| Favor-only | Shows up when they need something | Stop giving on demand |
| Curated persona | Amazing online, flaky in DMs | Compliment something specific, then a low-cost ask |
| Late-night only | "wyd" after 11, nothing before | Suggest daytime plans and see what happens |
| Template charm | Smooth lines, nothing about you | Detail test — see if they remember |
| Orbiter | Watches everything, never texts | Don't reward it; if they want you, they'll text |
| Situationship stall | Months in, acts like a couple, won't define it | Have the conversation, or decide your own deadline |
| Zombie | Ghosted, now back like nothing happened | Receive it lightly; make them earn a real plan |

### Test moves

These go in the strategy block or commentary, never inside a reply:

- **Detail test**: mention something only someone who was listening would remember; see if they pick it up.
- **Low-cost ask**: a light place + two specific time options; see if they commit.
- **Daylight test**: suggest a daytime plan; see if they still show up.
- **DM-to-plan test**: turn their story-reply flirting into "ok then, Thursday?"
- **Holiday test**: near a holiday or your birthday, see whether they make an actual plan.
- **Step back**: after a run of reaching out, go quiet for a few days; see if they come back.

When 2+ player signals hit, name the recent pattern and the next test in "Any chance?" or a complex Reply.

## 5. How this maps to the output contract

- Three layers → "Surface / Emotion / Need" in the judge block; slang and tone signals → "Note".
- Labels aren't facts + action calibration → the "Stage" line with its one piece of evidence.
- Four dimensions → the "Interest" line. The dimension rules still apply: high sweetness + low follow-through can't produce a high overall.
- Player score → the "Player" line, number only; band and evidence go in the commentary.
- Message type, risks and direction → the Decode commentary.
- Reality-check thresholds → the stop block.
